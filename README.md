# Xcel Time of Use Analysis

Xcel is changing their Time Of Use period and pricing, requiring a completely new analysis to determine which option is best for me. Xcel provides a calculator, but it is devoid of data and I don't really trust it.

So naturally, I worked with AI to generate a Jupyter Notebook to determine what I should do.

## Generating your CSV
1. Starting from https://my.xcelenergy.com/MyAccount/s/usage
2. Scroll down to the section called "My Energy Portal" and click the "Visit my energy" link
3. Click "View my usage & cost"
4. Change from "Month view" to "By hour"
5. Select an "Interval download" and pick whatever time frame you want. - I chose a 1-year period

## My Findings
Even with my 24/7 power hungry homelab, TOU will save me 1.8% based on last year's historical data.
